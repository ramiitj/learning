# Classroom context: India, classes 8–12

Source: `docs/13-ai-for-kids-v1-curriculum.md`, `docs/05` (classroom variants). These are the build team's working assumptions; validate them with pilot teachers.

- A period is 40 minutes; settling in and wrap-up take about 5 minutes together.
- Classes are often 40–60 students. Many schools have one projector or smart board; some have none. Students usually have no devices in class.
- Votes are by show of hands; the teacher counts roughly and records on the board.
- Teachers may teach bilingually (English with Hindi or Telugu). Many are not confident about AI and may worry about being asked questions they cannot answer.
- Classroom mode in the platform: the teacher projects one screen; each chapter title shares the screen with its first block; a clicker or Page Down moves one block at a time; votes use a show-of-hands tally.
- A teacher guide is one page (about 600 words) and is usable without having explored the platform.
