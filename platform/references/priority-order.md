# Priority order and conflict resolution

Source: constitution Articles 2–3, `docs/09-agent-operating-system.md`.

When considerations conflict, they are ranked: **safety, accuracy, clarity, experience, aesthetics.** The agent whose concern ranks higher prevails, and the agent whose concern ranks lower must find a way to serve its goal within that constraint. Example: if the experience designer wants to remove an explanation learners need, clarity prevails, and the designer's task becomes making that explanation delightful.

The orchestrator resolves what the order settles. Anything else goes to the creator's decision queue with both positions stated:
- both positions serve the same priority and cannot both be kept;
- the action is on the always-ask list;
- the request or a resolution contradicts the creator's stated intent;
- resolving would override a reviewer's safety or accuracy flag (the orchestrator may resolve such a flag only with a change that removes the problem).

Escalations are framed briefly, with both positions and at most three options.
