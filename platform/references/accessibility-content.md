# Reference: accessibility rules for lesson content (WCAG 2.2 AA, content side)

Used by: accuracy-safety-reviewer, learning-experience-designer, localisation-specialist.

- Never rely on colour alone: "the red ones", "the green button". Name things in words; colour can repeat the meaning.
- Never rely on position or shape alone: "the box on the left", "the round one".
- Never rely on sight alone: "look at the picture" needs the picture described in text.
- Every image has alt text that carries its teaching meaning; decorative images are marked decorative.
- Every video and audio element has captions and a transcript in every published locale; no autoplay.
- Instructions name the action and the control ("Tap 'Put it in Junk'"), not a gesture only.
- Numbers and symbols that are read aloud must make sense read aloud (explain ≥, ×, →).
