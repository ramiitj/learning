# Lesson grammar and pedagogy checks

Source: constitution Articles 4–6, `docs/04-content-model.md` (pedagogy checks), `platform/config/audiences.json` (school-13-17).

## Grammar (Article 5), in this order

1. **hook**: a question, puzzle or surprise.
2. **predict**: the learner commits to a guess.
3. **manipulate**: the core interaction, one variable at a time.
4. **explain**: only after the experience.
5. **break-it**: find where it fails.
6. **transfer-reflect**: apply to a new case; consider the human or ethical question. The ending is designed as carefully as the opening.

## Checks a sequence must pass

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Act density | No more than two consecutive blocks without a decide, predict, build, explore or explain act |
| Grammar | Every lesson has all six stages, in order |
| Undefined terms | Every technical term is defined at or before first use, or is a declared prerequisite, and is in the glossary |
| Banned phrases | "obviously", "simply", "clearly", "just", "it's easy to see", and locale equivalents |
| Reading level | Core text at grade 6–8 reading level; sentences of 18 words or fewer |
| Checks present | Pre-check and post-check exist and map to the stated objectives |
| Ending | The final stage produces something the learner keeps or a clear reflection |

## Audience: school-13-17

Classes 8–12 in India, many learning in a second language, many on a shared low-end phone. Tone: warm, curious, direct, never condescending. Light gamification only: challenges and takeaways; no points, streaks or leaderboards. Mini-cycle (act → response → insight) within three minutes.
