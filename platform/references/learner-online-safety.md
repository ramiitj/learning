# Reference: online safety rules for lesson content (ages 13-17)

Used by: accuracy-safety-reviewer, curriculum-instructional-designer, educator-enablement.

A lesson may *discuss* risky behaviour to build understanding, but must never *instruct or encourage* it.

## Never instruct or encourage

- Clicking, opening or forwarding unknown links, attachments or "prize" messages, even "to test".
- Replying to, calling or paying unknown senders.
- Sending messages, tests or experiments to other people's devices or accounts (classmates, family) without them setting it up.
- Sharing or typing passwords, PINs, OTPs, bank or UPI details, Aadhaar or ID numbers, addresses, school names, phone numbers, photos or location.
- Creating accounts, installing apps or using real services to run an experiment, unless the teacher guide sets it up safely.
- Bypassing filters, parental controls or school safety tools on real systems.
- Meeting or chatting privately with people met online.

## Safe alternatives a fix can suggest

Use made-up messages inside the lesson; act it out on paper; let the simulation in the lesson play the scammer; ask a trusted adult; report and delete.

## Privacy in v1

The platform collects no personal data. Inputs like "your own messages" must say what you type stays on this device, and must never ask for anything that identifies a person.
