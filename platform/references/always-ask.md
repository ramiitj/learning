# Autonomy levels

Source: `docs/10-governance-and-creator-console.md`.

- **Always ask** (nothing happens without the creator's explicit approval): publishing, unpublishing, deleting, production deploys, schema changes, new components, new agents, permission changes, constitution amendments, anything touching learner data, and any spend beyond the monthly budget.
- **Act and notify** (proceed; appears in the daily digest; reversible): draft revisions, analogy bank and glossary additions, fixes to validation flags.
- **Autonomous** (routine, logged): validation runs, simulated reviews, test runs, benchmarks, analytics reports.

No agent may publish, delete published content, deploy to production, change how learner data is handled, or change any profile, permission or the constitution.
